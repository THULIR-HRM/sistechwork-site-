Module 02 • Attendance Engine

Subject-Wise Attendance & Automated Alerts

Track daily physical and virtual lecture attendance with precision. Automatically compute present-to-total day ratios (46/50 days) with visual donut analytics and instant absent flags for proactive intervention.

  • High-Precision Ledger: Daily record categorized by date, course code, and faculty check-in timestamps.
  • Minimum Threshold Guard: Visual warnings when attendance nears institutional regulatory thresholds (75% / 85%).
  • Automated Guardian SMS/Email: Real-time broadcasts to parents regarding unexplained absences.

Module 03 • Academic Grading

Granular Internal & External Score Analysis

Eliminate confusion around grading breakdowns. TrainHub organizes internal assessments, mid-terms, and final external examinations into structured, verifiable scorecards with real-time performance tier badges.

  • Score Telemetry: Automatic calculation of top score (98/100), overall average (85.6%), and completion rate.
  • Multi-Component Weighting: Splits Internal (100) and External (100) exams with automatic status tiering.
  • Digital Marksheet Export: Download tamper-evident mark transcripts in high-res PDF or Excel formats with 1 click.
